FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often from property owners.

Do you offer free estimates?
Yes. Call, email, or use the contact form below and we'll schedule a free, no-obligation walkthrough of your property.
Are you licensed and insured?
Absolutely. Our crew is fully licensed and insured, so you're protected from the moment we arrive to the final cleanup.
How fast can you respond to storm damage or an emergency removal?
We prioritize urgent and hazardous situations and do our best to get a crew out quickly during business hours. Call us directly at (509) 846-5398 for the fastest response.
Do you handle stump grinding and debris cleanup?
Yes — stump removal and full site cleanup are part of our standard process, so you're left with a tidy, usable space.
Is trimming necessary if my tree already looks healthy?
Regular trimming prevents future problems — it improves airflow and sunlight, reduces storm-damage risk, and keeps a healthy tree healthy for longer.
What areas do you service?
We're based in Tonasket and serve Okanogan County and the surrounding areas — see our full list of service areas above.
